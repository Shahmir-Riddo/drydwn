1. What we collect

When you create an account, we store your username, email address, and password (hashed, never in plain text). Optional profile fields (display name, bio, location, avatar URL) are stored only if you choose to fill them in. Your wardrobe entries, ratings, and diary notes are stored to power the app's features.

2. Catalogue data is not personal data

The fragrance catalogue (names, houses, notes, and reference imagery) is reference material sourced from publicly available third-party sources, including Fragrantica (fragrantica.com), used here for personal, non-commercial, informational purposes. It is not user-submitted personal data, and DRYDOWN does not claim ownership of it. All trademarks, brand names, and imagery belong to their respective owners.

3. How we use your data

Your account data is used solely to operate DRYDOWN — authentication, displaying your wardrobe/diary, and search suggestions. We do not sell your data, and we do not share it with third parties for advertising.

4. Cookies & sessions

DRYDOWN uses session cookies to keep you signed in. No third-party tracking or advertising cookies are used.

5. Data retention & deletion

Your data is retained while your account is active. To request deletion of your account and associated data, contact the site operator using the details below.

6. Third-party content & takedowns

If you are a rights holder for any catalogue content (including images sourced from Fragrantica or other public listings) and want it removed, credited, or amended, contact the site operator. Valid requests will be honored promptly.

7. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated periodically. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above.
